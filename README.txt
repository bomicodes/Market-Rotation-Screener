v34.3 — SELLING CLIMAX vs DISTRIBUTION
- Each holding's bounce is read from its most recent swing low (last 30 sessions): was the low a selling climax
  (≥ 1.8× 50-day volume after a ≥ 5% decline)? Is the bounce getting up-day volume? Has a pullback tested the low on light volume?
- A group that reads as distribution over 20 days but where ≥ 25% of holdings printed a climax low becomes
  "Selling climax · testing the low" (≥ 20% testing) or "Selling climax · bounce has buying" — scoring then uses the since-the-low flow.
- Groups that stay "distribution" now say whether the bounce is on light volume (sellers stepping back) or has buying.
